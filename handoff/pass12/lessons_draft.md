Method lessons from adversarial review pass 12 (pinned `bd55f840`, record `docs/reviews/2026-10-12-pass12.md`), for the lever phase before pass 13 and the method document. Each is a change to the tooling, the briefs or the method, not a defect in pgpm.

1. `build_review_tree.sh` removes only `bench/mutations/`; the pass records under `docs/reviews/` stayed in the tree for the first ten minutes of the hunt (pass 11 stubbed them by hand). Four finders met them in a grep. The script should stub `docs/reviews/` the way it stubs the catalogue.
2. The slice line ranges must be generated AFTER the seeds are planted: planting shifts every unit past a seed's lines, and F4's ledger scored 43 of 62 until `mk_slices.py` was re-run on the seeded tree. Make `mk_slices.py` refuse a tree whose commit is not the sealed one, or have `plant_seeds.py` print the warning.
3. A seed that trips a repository lint is as loud as one the suite catches: the archive cursor's typmod seed made `scripts/check_archive_child_by_oid.py` refuse its line, so `bench/lint_child_by_oid_inverse.sh` and the Lint job were red on the seeded tree and four finders (F7, F11r, F12, F8r) reached it by running the lint. `plant_seeds.py --suite` should run the repository's lints beside the pgTAP suite and seal a lint-caught seed as such; the record then splits recall three ways.
4. `docs/adversarial-review.md` in the tree names the late-survivor set's shapes, and F17 read it and said the untransmute seed was "likely a seed". Either plant late-survivor seeds the document does not name by shape, or stub the "Seeding" section of the method document in the review tree.
5. The harness caps concurrent subagents at 20. With 19 finders, round-2 resumes and verifiers in flight the cap was met at 15:52Z and two re-runs did not launch; a launch queue (verifiers first, then round 2, then re-runs) kept the pipeline moving. Size the first wave at 15 or keep the queue.
6. A fresh-surface slice of FILES is sized by the files changed by more than a label, not by the files touched: S8c's 289 bench files were 30 new guards, 9 substantially changed, 50 lightly changed and 200 one-line label edits; its first finder read 30 whole, and the re-run on the 62 substantive files met the gate. The 197 label-only files were not re-run (budget); their home slices read them whole. Next pass: the fresh-surface bench and tests slices list only the substantively changed files, and hand the label-only edits as a diff to check, not as units.
7. Round 2 aimed at the six late-survivor shapes by name produced the round's Tier 1 (a hand regrain_step under REPEATABLE READ, from the finder's own hypothesis of shape 3) and both Tier 3 code findings (adopt_partition's snapshot reads, built independently by two finders; the cluster-wide backend_xmin); the labelling hypotheses the brief did not name produced four of the eight fell claims. Keep the shape list; drop the generic "build every labelling hypothesis" instruction for bench slices, or make the brief say a label is a claim only when a pipeline pairs the label with a verdict.
8. Three of the four Tier 1 root causes are OLD code found under a rotated lens (operator error: a REPEATABLE READ caller, a leading-slash prefix): the long tail the convergence rule expects. The fourth is YOUNG and sits on the lever phase's own surface: #1250 added the dispatch record and taught retire(), _detach_reap, _retain_recall and the install to read it, but not the three readers of `retiring_at` in the archive path (`_part_built`, `_part_detached_by_hand`, `_archive_hold_partition`), which the per-PR verification's widened scope ("every copy of a mechanism the diff touches") was meant to reach and did not: the mechanism is "who trusts a retirement mark", and the surface tool lists callers of touched units, not readers of a touched column. `pr_surface.py` should add the readers of every column the diff adds or changes.
9. Tier 4 remedies: five findings are refusal messages or documents whose remedy fails as typed (a `drop table` for a sequence, a hand drop that leaves a wedging row, a FINALIZE-less detach, a transmute re-run for from_hypertable, a label refusal that tells the operator to drop their own table). The operator-error lens's instruction to TYPE every remedy paid; carry it into the fix-phase acceptance: a fix PR for a refusal must run its own remedy.
